About This Service

Personalized Tutoring is Learnora's premium 1-on-1 academic support service. Rather than a one-size-fits-all approach, we tailor every session to the student's unique learning style, school curriculum, and current proficiency level.

This targeted approach allows students to ask questions freely, clarify doubts immediately, and build a strong foundation of conceptual understanding without the pressure of a large classroom.

Who Is It For?

Students Needing Extra Support

For those finding specific subjects challenging and requiring dedicated, patient help to catch up to their peers.

High Achievers

For advanced students wanting to push ahead of the curriculum and proactively prepare for highly competitive exams.
